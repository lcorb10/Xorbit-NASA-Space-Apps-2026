# Videos del manual (Google Flow / Veo)

Cada video dura unos 8 s, en 16:9 y sin texto en pantalla (el juego pone los textos en EN/ES).
Al recibir el MP4, guardarlo como `assets/video/<id>.ogv` (convertido con ffmpeg a Theora);
el manual muestra el botón "▶ Ver video" solo cuando el archivo existe.

| id | Dónde aparece |
|---|---|
| `build` | Cómo se arma un cohete |
| `staging` | Etapas |
| `twr` | Empuje / peso |
| `stability` | Centro de masa y de presión |
| `orbit` | La órbita |
| `isp` | Impulso específico |

## build — Cómo se arma un cohete
Clean educational 3D animation, white studio background with a faint blue engineering grid.
A two-stage rocket assembles itself from the bottom up, each part dropping into place with a
soft click: first a rocket engine nozzle, then four fins around its base, then a tall
cylindrical fuel tank, a thin separation ring, a smaller engine, a smaller tank, a payload
section with a satellite inside, and finally a pointed nose cone on top. Each part glows orange
for a moment as it lands. Slow orbiting camera, soft studio lighting, realistic metal materials,
no text, no logos.

## staging — Etapas
Realistic cinematic shot of a two-stage rocket climbing through the upper atmosphere at
sunset-blue sky. The first stage engine shuts off, small pyrotechnic bolts flash around the
separation ring, the empty first stage falls away tumbling slowly, and the second stage engine
ignites with a bright blue-white flame and accelerates. Camera follows alongside, tracking shot,
photorealistic, no text, no logos.

## twr — Empuje y peso
Slow-motion realistic shot of a rocket on a launch pad at night. Engines ignite with a huge
cloud of white steam, the rocket hesitates for a second as thrust builds, then lifts off slowly
and accelerates upward. Low camera angle from the ground, floodlights, photorealistic, no text,
no logos.

## stability — Estabilidad
Educational wind-tunnel style animation: two model rockets side by side in a stream of visible
air flow lines. The rocket with large fins at the bottom wobbles and straightens itself; the
rocket without fins tilts and flips over. Clean white background, side view, smooth motion,
no text.

## orbit — La órbita
Educational space animation: a cannon on top of a very tall mountain on Earth fires
cannonballs horizontally, each faster than the last; the slow ones curve down and hit the
ground, the fastest one falls around the whole planet and keeps circling (Newton's cannonball).
View from space, Earth with realistic clouds, stars in background, no text.

## isp — Impulso específico
Side-by-side comparison of two rocket engines firing on test stands: on the left a kerosene
engine with an orange sooty flame, on the right a hydrogen engine with an almost invisible blue
flame and visible shock diamonds. Static wide shot, realistic, daylight desert test site,
no text, no logos.
